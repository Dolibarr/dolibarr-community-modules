## Overview

Stancer is the French low-cost payment platform. The module connects it to
Dolibarr: your customers pay by card or by SEPA direct debit from their
invoices, orders and quotes, and Dolibarr records the payment, classifies the
invoice and books the bank entries without retyping anything.

The module is meant for companies and associations that bill in Dolibarr and
want to get paid online. It is free and released under a free software
licence; only the Stancer account has to be opened, on
[stancer.com](https://www.stancer.com/en/?mtm_campaign=Dolibarr&mtm_source=Dolibarr).

## Features

- **Card payment**: payment link sent by email from an invoice, an order or a
  quote, secure 3-D Secure page, card saved for the next payments, new attempt
  after a refusal
- **Quotes and orders**: automatic invoice creation, partial payment and down
  payment invoice
- **SEPA direct debit**: public IBAN entry page, mandate generated as a PDF,
  IBAN check through SEPAMail, automatic debit of due invoices after the
  customer notice period
- **Electronic signature of the mandate** with the UptoSign module; once the
  mandate is signed, the customer's template invoices switch to direct debit
- **SEPA rejections**: invoice reopened, customer and administrator notified,
  rejection fee invoiced if you wish
- **Tracking**: dashboard, payments, payouts, refunds and disputes
  synchronised with Stancer, Stancer tab on every thirdparty
- **Automation**: scheduled jobs and ready-to-use emails for every situation
- **Accounting**: fees recorded on the bank account, payouts booked as
  transfers to your main bank, discrepancies detected
- **Associations**: online payment of membership fees and donations
- **Six languages**: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Dutch
